RiskLens

Read corporate risk disclosures with traceable evidence

PE6201 · Zhang Yizhuo · Public English text only · Analyst reading assistance

1. Choose a disclosure


Paste Item 1A text. Separate up to six paragraphs with blank lines.



The LLM sends only this text to your configured provider. Do not paste confidential information. Severity describes the disclosure wording, not financial risk probability.

2. Review the evidence

Each paragraph returns a category, two summary sentences, a source quote and a review decision.

Confirm the original disclosure before using the result. A valid quote cannot prove the whole summary is correct.

3. Measured evaluation

Synthetic and real-source results are separated. No claimed 70% time saving. Full case outputs, errors and definitions are in the evals folder.
