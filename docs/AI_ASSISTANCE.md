# AI assistance and evidence boundaries

AI assistance was used to draft the initial proposal and, in this final build, to implement code, formulate the taxonomy and prompt, author synthetic fixtures, provisionally label short real excerpts, execute evaluation, and draft documentation and presentation materials. The student should review the reasoning, real-data labels and submission text before adopting the work.

The measured API outputs and costs are real calls to `openai/gpt-4o-mini` through OpenRouter. This differs from AI-authored narrative: statements about trials, human behaviour or independent judgement must have evidence. No independent hand labelling, observed analyst time savings, Zapier trial, 20-company collection or production deployment is claimed.

Data generated with AI and AI-assisted real labels are not independent ground truth. This limitation is stated in the report, dataset explainer and evaluation documentation. The student's face and voice in the demo must be the student's actual recording; no synthetic impersonation is used.
